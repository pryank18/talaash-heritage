# Heritage desk stories

One JSON file per story, named `<slug>.json`. Build with `python3 tools/build_news.py` (check only: `--check`). Pages: `site/news.html` and `site/news-<slug>.html`.

Fields: `slug`, `title` (8-160 chars, plain, no clickbait), `dek` (one or two sentences), `body` (2-8 paragraphs, our own words, no links), `category` (finds, research, exhibitions, events, opportunities, policy), `field` (archaeology, linguistics, anthropology, arts, sanskrit, history, heritage), optional `also_fields` (up to 2 more of the seven, when the story genuinely belongs there too), `published` (YYYY-MM-DD), optional `updated`, `place`, `event_start`, `event_end`, `deadline`, `correction`; `sources` (list of {title, publisher, url (https), date}); `status` (published, draft, withdrawn).

Rules: a story runs only if a primary source (PIB, ASI, ministry, museum, university, UNESCO, ICOMOS, the paper itself, the official notice) or two independent reputable outlets confirm it. Superlatives ("oldest", "first") are attributed to whoever claims them. At most one quote under 15 words. No copied headlines or images. First reported in the last 14 days, or an event or deadline at least 5 days away. Published stories are never deleted: correct them with `correction`, or set `status` to `withdrawn` with a note.

Coverage: the desk covers all seven fields in the name TALAASHH, not archaeology and history alone. Run `python3 tools/build_news.py --coverage` before choosing stories: it counts stories per field over the last 14 days (field plus also_fields) and prints PRIORITY fields with fewer than 2. Search those fields first, and pick the field by the subject of the story (an inscription's reading is linguistics as well as history; a dance festival is arts; a tribal community study is anthropology; a manuscript or museum decision is heritage; a Sanskrit text or Sanskrit event is sanskrit). Keep opportunities to at most a third of the stories in any 14 days. Never lower the sourcing rules to fill a field: a thin field stays thin rather than carry a weak story.

What counts in each field: archaeology (excavations, finds, dating, ASI and state digs); linguistics (languages, scripts, inscriptions read, dictionaries, endangered and tribal languages, language policy); anthropology (ethnography, tribal and nomadic communities, folk practice, physical anthropology, the Anthropological Survey of India); arts (painting, sculpture, crafts, music, dance, theatre, cinema heritage, biennales, art fairs, awards); sanskrit (Sanskrit, Prakrit and Pali texts, manuscripts, editions, Sanskrit institutions and events, Indian knowledge systems); history (historical research, archives, history conferences and posts); heritage (World Heritage, museums, conservation, intangible heritage, heritage law and policy, digitisation).
