# A painted rock shelter at Bhimbetka: 3D evidence sheet (arts)

Started 11 Nov 2026 by the Talaash 3D studio. **Status: sources chosen; no figure has been extracted or checked yet. Nothing on this sheet may be built until each figure is taken from the source named and passes the THREE CHECKS (render/3d/README.md).**

## The object
One painted shelter at Bhimbetka, Raisen district, Madhya Pradesh (UNESCO World Heritage Site, 2003), modelled as a rock overhang you can walk under, with the positions of painted panels marked and their documented colours shown as flat swatches. The paintings themselves are not redrawn unless a published tracing exists; each panel is otherwise a labelled outline.

## Sources to use (primary or standard works)
- Y. Mathpal, *Prehistoric Rock Paintings of Bhimbetka, Central India* (New Delhi, 1984): shelter numbers, plans, tracings, colours.
- V. S. Wakankar and R. R. R. Brooks, *Stone Age Painting in India* (1976).
- A published pigment analysis of Bhimbetka paintings (for example in *Purātattva* or a conservation journal), for the colours.
- UNESCO World Heritage List no. 925, Rock Shelters of Bhimbetka, and the ASI Bhopal Circle page.

## To extract (each with an evidence tag)
- Which shelter, its plan and the height of the overhang (Measured if a plan with scale exists, otherwise Estimated).
- Panel positions and sizes (Documented from the published plan or tracing).
- Colours used, with the pigment where analysed (Documented, from the pigment study).
- Dates as the sources give them, by phase (Documented).

## Rules
- No colour is shown that is not in a published study; a panel with no study is grey with the label "colour not recorded".
