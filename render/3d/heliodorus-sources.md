# Heliodorus pillar, Vidisha (Besnagar): 3D evidence sheet (linguistics / Sanskrit)

Started 11 Nov 2026 by the Talaash 3D studio. **Status: sources chosen; no figure has been extracted or checked yet. Nothing on this sheet may be built until each figure below is taken from the source named and passes the THREE CHECKS (render/3d/README.md).**

## The object
A stone pillar at Besnagar near Vidisha, Madhya Pradesh, with a Brahmi inscription naming Heliodorus, an envoy of the Indo-Greek king Antialkidas. Model: the shaft and capital as a stone you can turn, with the inscription's letters drawn after a published plate, letter by letter, and a transliteration panel.

## Sources to use (primary or standard works)
- J. Marshall, "Excavations at Besnagar", *Archaeological Survey of India Annual Report 1908-09* (and the 1913-14 report by D. R. Bhandarkar): pillar measurements, base excavation, photographs.
- D. C. Sircar, *Select Inscriptions Bearing on Indian History and Civilization*, vol. I (1942; 2nd ed. 1965): text and reading of the inscription.
- *Epigraphia Indica* or *Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society* plate of the inscription (find the clearest published plate and cite it by volume and page).

## To extract (each with an evidence tag)
- Height and section of the shaft (Measured, Marshall/Bhandarkar).
- Capital form (Documented, from the published photograph).
- Inscription: number of lines, position on the shaft, letter forms (Documented, plate); reading and translation (Documented, Sircar).
- Date as given by the sources (Documented; give the range the source gives, not a single year).

## Rules
- Letters drawn only after the published plate; any letter that cannot be read on the plate is shown as a gap, not guessed.
- No deity figure; the Garuda capital, if shown, as a plain labelled block unless a measured drawing exists.
