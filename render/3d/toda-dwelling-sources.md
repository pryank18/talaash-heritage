# Toda dwelling hut (Nilgiris): 3D evidence sheet (anthropology)

Started 11 Nov 2026 by the Talaash 3D studio. **Status: sources chosen; no figure has been extracted or checked yet. Nothing on this sheet may be built until each figure is taken from the source named and passes the THREE CHECKS (render/3d/README.md).**

## The object
The barrel-roofed dwelling hut of the Toda people of the Nilgiri hills, as recorded in published ethnography: a vernacular house type you can turn and cut open. Dwelling only. The Toda dairies are sacred places and are not modelled; no people are shown other than the plain 1.7 m scale figure.

## Sources to use (primary or standard works)
- W. H. R. Rivers, *The Todas* (London, 1906): chapter on the village and the hut, with drawings and photographs (public domain).
- M. B. Emeneau, published work on the Toda language and culture, for the names of the parts of the house (cite volume and page).
- Any later published survey of Toda houses (for example in the *Anthropological Survey of India* series) that gives measurements; cite it, and tag figures from it as Measured.

## To extract (each with an evidence tag)
- Overall length, width and height of the hut, and the size and height of the doorway (Measured, if a source gives them; otherwise Estimated with the reason).
- Materials of the roof, walls and front, and the building method (Documented).
- Internal arrangement as described (platform, hearth and so on) (Documented).

## Rules
- Shown with respect: no sacred buildings or rites, no identifiable people, no photographs of people reused.
- Language: describe the Todas in the present tense as a living community; say which period the source describes.
- Pages to point to: anthropology-and-tribal-india (sells), branches-of-anthropology.
