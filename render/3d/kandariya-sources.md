# Kandariya Mahadeva, Khajuraho: 3D evidence sheet

Copied 11 Nov 2026 from the project note "kandariya-3d-sources" (model: Design canvas aa5f80ed-5676-496a-aaee-353ea79aee60, "Kandariya Mahadeva Temple — 3D Reconstruction").

## Ground plan (the key source)
- J. Fergusson, *History of Indian and Eastern Architecture* (1910 ed.), fig. 342: "Plan of Kandarya Mahâdeva, Khajurâho (from a plan by Gen. Cunningham), scale 50 ft to 1 in". Public domain on Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1910_sketch_of_plan,_Kandariya_Mahadeva_Temple,_Khajuraho.jpg
- Scaled to the measured 109 ft length, the drawing gives 61 ft width against the stated 60 ft (1.7% off). Each axis is scaled to its own measured figure.
- Readings from the drawing: cell about 2.7 × 3.7 m; ambulatory 0.6–1.0 m wide; outer walls about 1.9 m thick; great-hall interior about 7.9 × 5.7 m with four central pillars plus two in each transept window; narrow mandapa and porch with balustrades.

## Measured
- 109 ft long, 60 ft wide externally; 116½ ft above ground and 88 ft above its own floor, so the floor is at 28½ ft (D. N. Shukla, *Vāstu-Śāstra*, 1960).
- The often-quoted 31 m height matches 35.5 m minus the 4 m terrace.

## Tower
- 84 urushringas round the main spire, so 85 peaks in all. Some accounts say "84 spires" as if that were the total.
- Urushringas in four tiers; saptaratha plan, seven projections per face. **Currently rests on Wikipedia "Urushringa" citing Harle: confirm in J. C. Harle, *The Art and Architecture of the Indian Subcontinent*, or drop.**
- Main spire: 12 storeys and 11 bhūmi-āmalakas (Krishna Deva, via a quoted summary: confirm or drop).

## Other details (Krishna Deva, ASI 1987/1990, via quoted summary: confirm)
- Five transepts; makara-toraṇa of four loops; marble liṅga on a sandstone pīṭhikā.
- Inscription naming king "Virimda" on a mandapa pilaster.

## Three checks, 11 Nov 2026 (for site/3d-kandariya-mahadeva.html)
Check 1 read Cunningham, ASI Reports vol. II (1871), pp. 419–420 (archive.org OCR), Shukla (1960) and UNESCO 240. Check 2 (sources given) and check 3 (independent, trying to disprove) ran as separate subagents.
- Kept: 109 ft long; about 59½ ft wide (Cunningham; Fergusson and Shukla give 60 ft); 116½ ft above the ground and 88 ft above its floor; plan order and the double cross with porticoes lighting the passage; stepped roofs to the spire; marble liṅga; 226 + 646 = 872 statues counted by Cunningham; largest standing temple at Khajuraho; Chandela dynasty, 10th–11th centuries CE.
- Dropped: 84 urushringas and four tiers (only a travel guide via Wikipedia), "Virimda" inscription (only a reference dictionary via Wikipedia; Cunningham found no inscription giving the date), five transepts and the makara-torana (no source found), 12 storeys and 11 bhūmi-āmalakas (no source reached), the 18 ft plinth (check 3 could not confirm), "mostly built 950–1050" (UNESCO dates the dynasty's apogee, not the building).
- Shown as Estimated: chamber sizes, wall and roof heights, smaller spires (number and grouping), amalaka and finial, terrace/base split.
