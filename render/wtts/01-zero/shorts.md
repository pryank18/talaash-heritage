Where did the word "zero" come from? | What the Text Says #01 #Shorts

## Description
English "zero" comes from Italian zero (directly or through French), from Medieval Latin zephirum, from the Arabic ṣifr, "empty". "Cipher" comes from the same Arabic word through Medieval Latin cifra. And ṣifr was itself the Arabic translation of the Sanskrit śūnya, which also means "empty".

Sources:
- Merriam-Webster, etymologies of "zero" and "cipher" (merriam-webster.com)

- V. S. Apte, The Practical Sanskrit-English Dictionary (Poona, 1890), p. 1052, s.v. śūnya
