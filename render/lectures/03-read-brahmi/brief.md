# Durlabh #03 · Read Brāhmī, a first lesson (Sun 6 Dec 2026) · Linguistics

This is the already-planned Talaash Lecture (formerly #1), now Durlabh #03. It teaches rather than reveals: Week 1 (Rāya Asoko) ends by pointing here, so open with those two words and teach the system they use.
Content: abugida principle (inherent a, vowel strokes, initial vowel letters), 10–12 high-frequency letters from Ashokan edicts, read one short real line (e.g. devānaṃpiya), practise drawing three letters.
Sources: Salomon, Indian Epigraphy (1998); Bühler, Indian Palaeography (1904; plates); Hultzsch, CII I (1925) for edict text; letter forms drawn after published plates and labelled.
