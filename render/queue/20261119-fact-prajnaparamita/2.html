How we know

Its long versions include the Hundred Thousand, Twenty-Five Thousand, Eighteen Thousand, Ten Thousand and Eight Thousand. The shortest holds it all in the syllable a; no Sanskrit copy of that one survives.

The Eight Thousand is probably the earliest. Lokakṣema’s Chinese version is dated 179 CE.

Sources: 84000, The Perfection of Wisdom in Eight Thousand Lines (Toh 12), introduction; 84000, The Perfection of Wisdom in One Syllable (Toh 23), tr. Lowell Cook.
