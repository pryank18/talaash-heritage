The panel

A king in a turban and large earrings, a parasol held over him, his queen beside him, attendants with the parasol and fly-whisks. Above them, one line:

𑀭𑀸𑀬𑀸 𑀅𑀲𑁄𑀓𑁄
rāyā asoko · ‘King Ashoka’

Reading of the scholarly edition (often printed rāya). Typed in a modern Brāhmī font, not traced from the stone.
