A Sanskrit fellowship at Oxford

The Queen’s College, Oxford, seeks a John P. Clay Career Development Fellow in Sanskrit at its Centre for Manuscripts and Text Cultures: three years from 1 September 2027, salary £43,690 a year.

You need a doctorate in Sanskrit or a closely related field by 1 June 2027. Apply online by 8 February 2027.

Source: The Queen’s College, Oxford, vacancy page and further particulars.
