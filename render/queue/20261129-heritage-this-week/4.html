10,102 documents, one search page

DHARMA, a European Research Council project on sources from South and Southeast Asia, ran from May 2019 to October 2026. It leaves the database dharmalekha.info, mostly inscriptions.

On 23 November its search page listed 10,102 documents, 5,128 of them in Tamil and 2,043 in Sanskrit. A consortium now runs it.

Sources: dharmalekha.info; INDOLOGY mailing list, 20 November 2026.
