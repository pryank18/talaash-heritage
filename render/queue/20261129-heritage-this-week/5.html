Sanskrit Wikisource in one download

Roberto de Andrade Martins has converted the Devanagari texts of Sanskrit Wikisource to IAST: 2,653 works in one download of about 135 MB on the Internet Archive.

The conversion was done by script, and no one says it was checked by hand. Before you quote a passage, check it against a printed edition.

Sources: Internet Archive record; INDOLOGY mailing list, 19 November 2026.
