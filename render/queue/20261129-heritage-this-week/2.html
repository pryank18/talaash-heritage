At least $80,000 a year for a recent PhD

The Ludo and Rosane Rocher Foundation is taking applications for its postdoctoral fellowship in classical Indology: up to 24 months at a host institution in the United States.

You need a doctorate in a relevant field of classical Indology, from an accredited university in any country, completed within five years before the fellowship begins. Apply by 15 January 2027.

Source: Ludo and Rosane Rocher Foundation.
