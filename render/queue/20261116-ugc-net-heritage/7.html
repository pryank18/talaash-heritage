Not registered? The rules this cycle

A master’s with 55% (50% for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD and third gender). Final-year master’s students may apply provisionally and must pass within two years of the result.

For JRF, the age limit is 30 on 1 December 2026, with up to 5 years’ relaxation for some groups.

A 4-year bachelor’s with 75% allows JRF and PhD admission only, not Assistant Professor. No upper age limit for Assistant Professor. Source: NTA Information Bulletin.
