How the 6% cut works

Slots for Assistant Professor equal 6% of candidates who sit both papers, split by category under the reservation policy.

To be counted you need at least 40% aggregate across both papers (General and EWS) or 35% (OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD, third gender). Cut-offs are then set for each subject and category.

Multi-shift subjects use normalised marks. Source: NTA Information Bulletin.
