What it says

Part of the inscription, in Mauryan Brahmi. Plate from V. A. Smith (1924), public domain.

It includes the words salilanidhane Budhasa Bhagavate Sakiyanam.

Georg Bühler’s reading: the casket with the relics of the Lord Buddha was given by the Sukirti brothers, with their sisters, sons and wives, of the Sakya clan.
