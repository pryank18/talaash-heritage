Where the finds are now

The Indian Museum, Kolkata, holds the casket and dates it to about the 3rd century BCE.

Further ASI excavations in 1971–77 found more caskets with 22 bone relics, now in the National Museum, New Delhi.

In May 2025 India halted a Sotheby’s auction in Hong Kong of part of the Piprahwa relics. They came back on 30 July 2025.

Sources: Indian Museum, Kolkata, record “Inscribed Relic Casket from Piprahwa” (archived); PIB, “A Joyous Homecoming”, 5 August 2025.
