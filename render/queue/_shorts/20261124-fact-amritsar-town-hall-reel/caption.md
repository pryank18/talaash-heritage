## Caption
Amritsar's colonial Town Hall now houses the Partition Museum, a short walk from Harmandir Sahib.

The Partition Museum dates the red-brick Town Hall to 1870. Today the building houses the museum, which collects objects, letters, photographs and oral histories of 1947. The museum marks 17 August, the day in 1947 the new borders were announced, as Partition Remembrance Day.
