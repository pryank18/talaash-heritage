## Caption
Jallianwala Bagh ki yeh deewar ek dastavez hai.

Site par lagi plaque ke mutabik is deewar par goliyon ke 28 nishan hain. Har nishan ko naqshe par BB1 se BB28 tak number diya gaya hai, aur deewar par safed khane mein ghera gaya hai. Archaeology mein evidence aise hi record hota hai.

13 April 1919, Baisakhi ke din, Brigadier-General Reginald Dyer ke aadesh par bina chetavni goliyan chalayi gayin. Saikdon log maare gaye aur ghayal hue.

In English: the site's plaque records 28 bullet marks on this wall, numbered BB1 to BB28 and boxed in white on the brick. On 13 April 1919, the day of Baisakhi, troops under Brigadier-General Reginald Dyer fired without warning on the crowd in the Bagh, killing and wounding many hundreds of people.
