Lamps for a light that had gone

The Kalpa Sutra says that on the new-moon night Mahavira attained nirvana, the kings of Kasi and Kosala made an illumination:

“Since the light of intelligence is gone, let us make an illumination of material matter!”

Kalpa Sutra, Life of Mahavira 128, tr. H. Jacobi (1884).
