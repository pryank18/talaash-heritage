25 July to 20 November

In the Panchang, Vishnu goes to sleep on Devshayani Ekadashi in Ashadha and wakes four months later on Prabodhini Ekadashi, also called Dev Uthani or Devutthana Ekadashi.

This year, for New Delhi: Devshayani fell on 25 July; Devutthana is today, 20 November, 118 days later by our count. Chaturmas, the holy four months, begins on Devshayani.

Source: Drik Panchang, Devshayani and Prabodhini Ekadashi 2026 (New Delhi). Drik also lists a second, Gauna Devutthana Ekadashi on 21 November.
