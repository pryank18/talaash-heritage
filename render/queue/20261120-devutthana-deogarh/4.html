The many-hooded serpent shades the sleeping god. Lakshmi is by his foot. At the top, Brahma sits on a lotus, with Indra on his elephant and Shiva and Parvati on Nandi.

The temple dates from the early 6th century, in the Gupta period. This scene is on its south wall; the north shows Gajendra’s rescue, the east Nara and Narayana.

Sources: Smarthistory, Dashavatara Temple, Deogarh (from The MAP Academy); British Library, Photo 1003/(751).
