Found near Kucha

Local treasure-seekers dug into an old site near Kucha, an oasis town on the Silk Road in what is now western China.

Among their finds was a bundle of birch-bark leaves. A British officer, Lieutenant H. H. Bower, obtained it, and it still carries his name.

Sources: Bodleian Libraries, MS. Sansk. c. 17; A. F. R. Hoernle, Proceedings of the Asiatic Society of Bengal, April 1891.
