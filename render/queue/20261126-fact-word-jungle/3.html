A word from medicine

Hobson-Jobson notes that the Sanskrit word occurs chiefly in medical treatises. Suśruta sorts land into humid, jaṅgala and ordinary: jaṅgala is the dry kind.

In strictness, it says, the Indian word meant only waste, uncultivated ground.

Source: Yule and Burnell, Hobson-Jobson, entry JUNGLE.
