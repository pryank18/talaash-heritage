Where it went

Kaithi wrote Bhojpuri, Magahi, Maithili, Awadhi and Urdu. In 1963 a Bihar gazetteer for Purnea called it “in vogue but declining in use”.

Since 2009 it has had its own Unicode block, so it can be typed today.

Anshuman Pandey, Proposal to Encode the Kaithi Script (2007), pp. 1, 13-17, 23; Unicode 5.2.
