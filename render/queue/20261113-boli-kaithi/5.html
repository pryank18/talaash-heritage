Won, lost, won, overtaken

1880 Lieutenant-Governor Ashley Eden orders Kaithi alone for Bihar’s courts.

1881 Persian is barred. Kaithi or Devanagari are allowed in court.

1893 Bengal cancels the order under pressure from Devanagari’s supporters.

1894 The cancellation is reversed. Kaithi is back.

1913 Kaithi is still official. After this, Devanagari is preferred.
