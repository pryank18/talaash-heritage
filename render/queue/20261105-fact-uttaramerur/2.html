How we know

Inscriptions of about 919–921 CE, under the Chola king Parantaka I, record how the local assembly chose its committees.

Names went on palm-leaf tickets in a pot, and a young boy drew them.

Source: R. Thapar, Early India; R. Nagaswamy, Uttaramerur (2003).
