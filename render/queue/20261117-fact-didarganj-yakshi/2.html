Talaash Heritage · Fact 0112 / 3

How we know

Bihar Museum’s record gives two accounts: a letter to E.H.S. Walsh, Commissioner of Patna, credits Ghulam Rasul; a police report of 20 October 1917 tells of a washermen’s slab, a snake and villagers digging.

A Smarthistory essay names another finder on 18 October 1917.

Sources: Bihar Museum, object record Arch. 134; Smarthistory, essay on the Didarganj Yakshi.
