An island in a tank

He found the tomb on an island in a large tank at the west end of the town. A bridge from the north stair had once led to it. It had fallen.

“The only access now is by a raft made of earthen pots,” built only when visitors came out of curiosity.

Source: Buchanan, Shahabad, pp. 92–94.
