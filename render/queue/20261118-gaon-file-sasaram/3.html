The record

Francis Buchanan surveyed Shahabad district in the dry season of 1812–13, part of his survey of the Bengal Presidency, 1807–14.

His full report was first printed in 1934, at Patna, by the Bihar and Orissa Research Society, from the Buchanan manuscripts in the India Office Library.

Source: Francis Buchanan, An Account of the District of Shahabad in 1812-13 (Patna, 1934), title page, foreword and p. 1.
