The father’s tomb, a military depot

In the middle of the town stood the tomb Sher Shah built for his father. On the arrival of a military detachment, it had lately been seized as a depot.

The mosque beside it, still used for prayer, was filled with grain. Buchanan wrote that he visited “with disgust and shame”.

Source: Buchanan, Shahabad, pp. 91–92.
