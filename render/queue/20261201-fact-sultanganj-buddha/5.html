Talaash Heritage · Fact 0175 / 5

The record: 1885A1116

Height 223 cm. Dated 500–700 CE. Cast by the lost-wax method on a clay core. The museum’s record calls it “the largest known complete Indian bronze sculpture”.

Sources: Birmingham Museums & Art Gallery, BMAGiC record 1885A1116 (archived); BBC Birmingham, “The Sultanganj Buddha”, 2006.
