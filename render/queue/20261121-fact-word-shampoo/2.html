Chāmpo: “press!”

Hindi and Urdu चाँपो (chāmpo) is the command form of चाँपना (chāmpnā), “to press, to massage”.

Hobson-Jobson, the old glossary of Anglo-Indian words, calls shampoo “most probably a corruption” of chāmpo. English borrowed the word for the practice: kneading and pressing the muscles.

Sources: Merriam-Webster, shampoo; Yule and Burnell, Hobson-Jobson, entry SHAMPOO.
