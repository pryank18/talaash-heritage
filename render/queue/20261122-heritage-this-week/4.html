Oxford’s manuscript catalogue, searchable

Suhas Mahesh has put the handwritten catalogue of the Chandra Shum Shere manuscripts at Oxford online, searchable by title, author and shelfmark. He puts it at about 7,000 manuscripts.

The site labels the text machine transcription without human editing. Before you cite an entry, check it against its linked page image.

Sources: suhasmahesh.com; INDOLOGY mailing list, 13 November 2026.
