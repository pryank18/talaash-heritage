A permanent post teaching Hindi

Uppsala University, Sweden, seeks a Senior Lecturer in Hindi in its Department of Linguistics and Philology. The post is permanent, starting with a temporary contract, and full time.

You need a doctorate in Indology or another relevant field of language studies, and are expected to be able to teach in Swedish within two years. Apply by 11 January 2027, reference UFV-PA 2026/2814.
