## Caption
Gurpurab today: the 557th Prakash Purab of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, from Harmandir Sahib, Amritsar.

Guru Nanak Dev Ji's birth anniversary is kept on Kartik Purnima, which falls today, 24 November. We spent a day at Harmandir Sahib in Amritsar this month.

The shrine stands in the Amrit Sarovar, the pool that gives the city its name. It was completed under Guru Arjan Dev Ji in 1604 and rebuilt after its destruction in the 18th century; its upper storeys were covered in gilded copper under Maharaja Ranjit Singh (r. 1801–39).

Over a gilded gateway runs a line of Gurbani from the Ramkali ki Vaar of Satta and Balwand, minstrels at the court of Guru Arjan Dev Ji, on Ang 968 of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji: "Dhan dhan Ramdas Gur, jin siriya tinai savariya". In our rendering: "Blessed, blessed is Guru Ram Das; the One who made you has adorned you."
