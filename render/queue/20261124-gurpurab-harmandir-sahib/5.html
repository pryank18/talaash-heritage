The line over the gateway

Dhan dhan Ramdas Gur, jin siriya tinai savariya. Our rendering: ‘Blessed, blessed is Guru Ram Das; the One who made you has adorned you.’

It is from the Ramkali ki Vaar of Satta and Balwand, minstrels at the court of Guru Arjan Dev Ji, on Ang 968 of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji.

Source: Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Ang 966–968.
