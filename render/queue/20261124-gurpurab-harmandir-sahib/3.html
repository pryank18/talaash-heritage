The shrine in the pool

Harmandir Sahib stands in the Amrit Sarovar, the pool from which Amritsar takes its name.

It was completed under Guru Arjan Dev Ji, the fifth Guru, in 1604, and rebuilt after its destruction in the 18th century. Its upper storeys were covered in gilded copper under Maharaja Ranjit Singh (r. 1801–39).

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica, ‘Golden Temple’.
