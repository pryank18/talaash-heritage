Sell with us

For guides, archaeologists, historians, museum educators, conservators and makers who want their walks, courses, counselling sessions or prints in front of people who care about India's past.

How it works

  1. ApplyTell us who you are and what you want to offer. We reply within two working days.
  2. Add your listingsOnce approved, sign in on the partner page, add a walk, course or session with its price, date and seats. We check each listing before it goes live.
  3. We take the bookingsBuyers pay Talaash Heritage by UPI. You see each paid booking with the buyer's contact details on your partner page.
  4. You get paid every MondayWe send your share by UPI: the booking amount minus 20% commission.

Partner terms

  • Commission. 20% of each paid booking, unless we agree a different rate when approving you. The rate in force when a booking is made applies to it.
  • Payouts. Every Monday, by UPI to the ID you give us, for bookings paid up to then. Refunds come out of your share; if a refund follows a payout, it is taken from the next one.
  • Your listings. You run what you list, as described, on the date listed. You are responsible for permits, site and museum rules, safety, and the accuracy of what you teach.
  • Cancellations. Buyers can cancel under our refund policy. If you cancel a session, every buyer is refunded in full and nothing is owed to you for it.
  • Buyers' details. Use buyers' names and contact details only to run the booking. Do not add them to your own lists or take payment from them outside this site for bookings made here.
  • Review. We check every new listing and every change before it goes live, and can pause listings that do not match what was described.
  • Leaving. Either side can end the arrangement at any time. Bookings already paid are still run, and earnings already owed are still paid.
  • Taxes. You are responsible for tax on your earnings.
