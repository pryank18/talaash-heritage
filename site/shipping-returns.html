Shipping, cancellations and refunds

Last updated October 2026.

Shipping

  • We ship physical items within India only.
  • Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 3 to 5 working days. Delivery usually takes a further 3 to 7 days depending on your PIN code.
  • Delivery costs ₹80 per order and is free on orders of ₹1,500 or more.
  • We email the tracking number when the parcel leaves us.

Recordings

Recordings are delivered instantly, so they cannot be returned. If a link does not work, tell us and we will fix it or refund you.

Heritage walks and experiences

Full refund if you cancel 72 hours or more before the start. After that we cannot refund, but you may send someone in your place.

Counselling sessions

You can move your slot free of charge up to 24 hours before it. If you have paid and not yet booked a slot, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days. A missed session without notice is not refunded.

Books, prints and other items

  • If your parcel arrives damaged or wrong, email us a photograph within 7 days of delivery. We will replace it or refund you, including delivery.
  • We do not take back items for a change of mind.

How refunds are paid

UPI payments are refunded to the UPI ID they came from. Card payments go back to the card. Either way, within 5 to 7 working days of our confirming the refund.
