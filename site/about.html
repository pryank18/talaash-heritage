About Talaash Heritage

Talaash means search. The name also spells out who we are: a Team for Archaeology, Linguistics, Anthropology, Arts, Sanskrit, History and Heritage. We started in 2021.

We teach from primary sources. A session on Aśoka starts with the edicts. A session on temples starts with a plan and an elevation. We would rather you leave able to read one inscription than able to repeat ten dates.

What we run

  • Lectures online or at your venue, from one hour to an evening.
  • Courses in languages, scripts, architecture and field methods, run for a batch on dates you choose.
  • Heritage walks and museum visits in Delhi, with an archaeologist or historian.
  • Hands-on workshops, from a practice excavation to preventive conservation.
  • Field schools and study tours of several days, with a day-by-day itinerary shared before you commit.
  • One-to-one counselling for people who want to study or work in archaeology, history and heritage.
  • Recordings and printed study aids for people who prefer to learn at their own pace.

Arranged for your group

Almost everything we run is arranged on request. Choose a programme from the catalogue as a starting point and send us your preferred dates, group size and city. We adapt the length, level, language and venue. The group size on each listing is a guide, not a requirement. No payment is taken until you approve the plan.

Our vision

To tell the stories of India's culture and heritage in new ways, create work for the people who care for it, and build a community of citizens who protect what has been passed down to us. Students are the future caretakers of India's heritage, so we work to make the subject not just educational but enjoyable.

Who it is for

Students preparing for history and archaeology programmes, guides, teachers, and anyone curious enough to give a Saturday to an inscription. Sessions are in English, Hindi or both; each listing says which.

For schools and colleges

We run culture and heritage programmes for students, from a single lecture to a full term of activities.

Lectures

  • Culture and heritage
  • Careers in archaeology, linguistics, anthropology and museums
  • What archaeology is: how sites are found, explored and excavated, and what happens to them after
  • Human evolution, the Stone Age and the first settlements
  • The early civilisations

Heritage tours

  • Visits to historical sites
  • Meeting communities and learning about their ways of life and food
  • Museum and art gallery visits

Workshops

  • Pottery making
  • Sculpture: a talk, a visit to a temple, monastery or museum to study sculpture, and a quiz

Students receive certificates, meet academics and field experts, and get practical experience beyond the classroom.

Each of these can be built into a single visit or a term-long plan. See every programme, or tell us the class and the syllabus and we will suggest one.

Common questions

Is there a minimum group size?

No. Each listing suggests a group size suited to the format. It is a guide, not a requirement. Tell us your numbers and we will advise whether the format suits them and what it would cost.

Can individuals or small groups make a request?

Yes. Most programmes are arranged for whoever requests them, whatever the number. When we schedule a public date that anyone can join, it appears in the catalogue with its date, price and seats.

How is the price calculated?

Each quotation is prepared for your group. It depends on how many people come, how long the programme runs, where it happens and whether anyone has to travel. You receive a written quotation that states what is covered and what is not, such as entry tickets, transport and meals. No payment is taken until you approve it.

Can the programme be changed to suit us?

Yes. Length, level and language are agreed with you. The same subject can be taught to Class 6 or to a postgraduate department. You can also combine programmes: a lecture with a walk, a workshop with a museum visit, a course with a field trip.

Where do programmes take place?

Lectures and most workshops run at your school, college or office, or online. Courses run online. Walks and museum visits are in Delhi. Study tours and field schools take place at the sites they study. If an expert has to travel to reach you, the quotation shows that cost.

What do we see before committing to a study tour or field school?

A day-by-day itinerary and a quotation. Route, pace, accommodation and budget are adjusted until they suit your group, and you commit only after that.

Who leads the sessions?

Archaeologists, historians, epigraphists, conservators and other specialists who work in the subject they teach. Each listing names the kind of specialist who leads it, and the individual is confirmed once your date is fixed.

What happens in bad weather or if a site is closed?

We may postpone a walk for weather or a site closure. You are offered the new date or a refund.

Do students receive certificates?

Yes. Students on our school and college programmes receive certificates.

What if we have to cancel?

See the cancellation and refund policy. If your dates change, tell us as early as you can and we will look for a new date first.
