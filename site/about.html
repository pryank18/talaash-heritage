About Talaash Heritage

Talaash means search. The name also spells out who we are: a Team for Archaeology, Linguistics, Anthropology, Arts, Sanskrit, History and Heritage. We started in 2021.

We teach from primary sources. A session on Aśoka starts with the edicts. A session on temples starts with a plan and an elevation. We would rather you leave able to read one inscription than able to repeat ten dates.

What we run

  • Lectures online or at your venue, from one hour to an evening.
  • Courses in languages, scripts, architecture and field methods, run for a batch on dates you choose.
  • Heritage walks and museum visits in Delhi, with an archaeologist or historian.
  • Hands-on workshops, from a practice excavation to preventive conservation.
  • Field schools and study tours of several days, with a day-by-day itinerary shared before you commit.
  • One-to-one counselling for people who want to study or work in archaeology, history and heritage.
  • Recordings and printed study aids for people who prefer to learn at their own pace.

Arranged around you

Almost everything we run is arranged on request. Take a programme from the catalogue as a starting point and tell us your dates, your group and your city. We set the length, level, language and venue to suit. The group size on each listing is a guide, so ask whatever your numbers. Nothing is charged until you approve the plan.

Our vision

To tell the stories of India's culture and heritage in new ways, create work for the people who care for it, and build a community of citizens who protect what has been passed down to us. Students are the future caretakers of India's heritage, so we work to make the subject not just educational but enjoyable.

Who it is for

Students preparing for history and archaeology programmes, guides, teachers, and anyone curious enough to give a Saturday to an inscription. Sessions are in English, Hindi or both; each listing says which.

For schools and colleges

We run culture and heritage programmes for students, from a single lecture to a full term of activities.

Lectures

  • Culture and heritage
  • Careers in archaeology, linguistics, anthropology and museums
  • What archaeology is: how sites are found, explored and excavated, and what happens to them after
  • Human evolution, the Stone Age and the first settlements
  • The early civilisations

Heritage tours

  • Visits to historical sites
  • Meeting communities and learning about their ways of life and food
  • Museum and art gallery visits

Workshops

  • Pottery making
  • Sculpture: a talk, a visit to a temple, monastery or museum to study sculpture, and a quiz
  • Languages: Prakrit, Sanskrit and more
  • Mock trench: a practice excavation with real methods and planted finds
  • Documentation, on site and in class

Students receive certificates, meet academics and field experts, and get practical experience beyond the classroom.

Each of these can be built into a single visit or a term-long plan. See every programme, or tell us the class and the syllabus and we will suggest one.
