Founder and writer

@archaeologywala

Archaeology and history, explained from the evidence. @archaeologywala is the channel of Pryank Wadhera, who founded Talaash Heritage and writes the guides on this site.

Follow on Instagram Career courses he teaches

Background

  • MA in Ancient Indian History, Culture and Archaeology, Deccan College Post-Graduate and Research Institute, Pune (2019).
  • BA (Honours) in History, College of Vocational Studies, University of Delhi (2017).
  • Began Talaash Heritage with colleagues in archaeology, linguistics and history in 2018; it was registered in 2021. See the field projects and research.

What he writes

The Learn section of this site: guides to Indian history, archaeology, temple architecture, Sanskrit and scripts, anthropology, and heritage walks across India. Each guide is written for a curious reader and checked against the sources it describes. If you spot an error, tell us and we correct it.
