Privacy notice

Version 2026-10-v1. Last updated October 2026.

This notice explains what Talaash Heritage collects when you register on this site or place an order, what we do with it, and what you can ask us to do. You can read this page without registering.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy we need it
Name, email address, phone numberTo create your registration, send lecture links, receipts and order updates, and reach you if a session changes.
City, state, country and PIN codeTo plan walks and in-person sessions where our audience is, and to deliver parcels.
Precise location, only if you press "Add my precise location"The same purpose as your city. It is rounded to about 100 metres before it leaves your device. You can skip it.
IP address, approximate country, browser type, the page you arrived on and the site that sent youTo stop spam registrations and to understand which posts and links bring people here.
Delivery address, what you ordered and the UPI reference you give usTo match your payment to your order, fulfil it, and keep the records the law requires of a seller.
What you tell us before or during a counselling sessionTo advise you. It is seen only by the person counselling you and is not used for anything else.

UPI payments go straight from your app to our account. We see what any UPI recipient sees: your name, the amount and the reference number. We never see or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords. Where card payment is offered, it is handled by Razorpay.

Announcements

We send announcements about new lectures, courses and walks by email and WhatsApp only if you tick that box when you register. Every message tells you how to stop. Messages about an order or a session you booked are sent regardless, because you need them.

Who else handles your details

  • Supabase hosts our database.
  • Razorpay, only if you choose card payment, processes it and receives your name, email, phone and order amount.
  • Our calendar and video-call providers receive your name and email when you book a counselling slot or join a session.
  • Our web host serves this site and, like any host, sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Courier companies receive your name, phone and address when we ship a parcel.
  • Partners who run a session you booked receive your name, phone, email and, for parcels, your address, once your payment is confirmed. They may use them only to run that booking.

We do not sell your details or pass them to advertisers.

If you sell with us

Partners give us their name, email, phone, city, a description of what they offer, a short public bio and a UPI ID for payouts. Their name, city and bio appear on their listings. The rest is used to review the application, contact them and pay them. Their password is stored only in scrambled form.

How long we keep it

Registration details are kept until you ask us to delete them, or for three years after your last visit or order, whichever comes first. Order and payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the details we hold, correct them, delete them, or stop announcements. Withdrawing consent is as simple as giving it: email talaashheritage@gmail.com from your registered address and we will act within seven days. You can also update your details yourself from the link at the foot of any page.

Deleting your registration does not delete invoices for past orders, which we must keep.

Children

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to register and order for you.
